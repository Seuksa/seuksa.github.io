> មាន​មនុស្ស​ទិញ​ឆ្នោត​២​០​ ​ដ​ល់់​៣​០​ឆ្នាំ​ ​តែ​នៅតែ​ក្រ​។​ ​នេះ​បញ្ជាក់​ថា​ ​ចង់​មាន​ត្រូវ​ចេះ​សន្សំ​ ​និង​ចេះ​រកស៊ី​ទៅ​។​

For years on end there have been people who have bought lottery tickets, but they are still empty-handed dirt-poor. This is to prove that if you want to prosper, you have to work hard. There is no such a thing as a freebie.
