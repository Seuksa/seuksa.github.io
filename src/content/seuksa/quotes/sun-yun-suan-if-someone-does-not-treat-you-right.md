> បើ​មាន​មនុស្ស​ណាមួយ​ប្រាស្រ័យ​នឹង​កូន​ៗ​ ​មិនបាន​ល្អ​ ​កុំ​រវល់​នឹង​ពួកគេ​នាំឲ្យ​តែ​ខាត​ពេលវេលា​ទេ​ ​។​ ​គ្មាន​អ្នកណា​ត្រូវធ្វើ​ល្អ​នឹង​យើង​ ​ក្រៅពី​ពុក​ម៉ែ​ទេ​នៅ​លើ​លោក​នេះ​ ​។​

If someone does not treat you right, don’t waste your time taking that to heart. In this life, no one has the obligation to treat you well except your parents. If there is someone who treats you right, apart from being grateful and appreciative, you should be a little cautious because in life people tend to have an ulterior motive for all their actions. So do not rush into the conclusion that they are your best friends.
