> កូន​ៗ​អាច​​​រក្សា​ពាក្យ​សម្ដី​នឹង​មនុស្សម្នា​ជុំវិញ​ខ្លួន​។​ ​តែ​កុំ​រំពឹង​ថា​ ​គេឯង​នឹង​រក្សា​ពាក្យ​សម្ដី​នឹង​កូន​ៗ​។​ ​កូន​ៗ​អាច​ធ្វើអំពើ​ល្អ​ឲ្យ​មនុស្សម្នា​បាន​។​ ​តែ​កុំ​រំពឹង​ថា​ ​គេឯង​នឹង​ធ្វើអំពើ​ល្អ​ដាក់​កូន​ៗ​វិញ​នោះ​។​

You can ask yourself to keep your trustworthiness, but you cannot request others to show trustworthiness towards you. You can tell yourself to treat people well, but you cannot expect other people to treat you well. However you treat people does not mean that people will return your kindness. If you don’t understand this fact, you are bound to reap untold disappointment for yourself.
