> អំណាច​ ​បុណ្យសក្តិ​ ​មិន​អាច​ស្មើ​រ​កេរ្តិ៍ឈ្មោះ​ ​និង​កិត្តិយស​បានទេ​។​ ​រៀន​ស្គាល់​តម្លៃ​នៃ​វត្ថុ​រូបិយ​ ​និង​អ​រូបិយ​… ​កុំ​រៀន​ដឹង​វត្ថុ​នោះ​មាន​តម្លៃ​ប៉ុន្មាន​ដុល្លារ​?​

I do not ask any of you to take care of me during the last part of my lifespan. For the same token, don’t expect me to take care of the second portion of your existence. When you all are growing up and have become independent, I have done my sacred duty. Later on whether you use the public transportation or drive your own luxury car, whether you eat shark-fin soup or Ramen noodle, it’s all up to you.

(សម្គាល់៖ ការបកប្រែមានលក្ខណៈប្រយោល)
