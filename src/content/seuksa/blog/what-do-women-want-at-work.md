I have read a infographic on LinkedIn blog about "What do Women want? at work.." which is focusing on other countries than Cambodia but those questions should be answered as well to our women in Cambodia.

Of course, people is working for money but in fact, it was not exactly, we might work for some reasons which is more meaningful than money we get. In the infographic said that most of women work for a balance between work and personal life; and in the past, women considered to work for earning a high salary.

I believe it's hard to compare to our Cambodians since we are in developing, economics is more important but let's try to find out.

So between:

1. Finding the right balance between work and personal life
2. Earning a high salary

What is the most value, do you consider the most, as a Cambodian married or single woman?

And how do you think about these three questions:

1. What do you want at work?
2. What does success mean to you?
3. What is your biggest challenge at work?

I do hope most women could give your opinion in this blog comment.

Let's welcome upcoming "International Women Right Day"!
