Technology is required now to make education more easier and faster.

By Phnom Penh Post:

Smartphones and tablets may find a place in the Cambodian classroom, as the launch of a new pilot program by two NGOs aims to tackle illiteracy with e-learning in schools.

In October, [World Education](http://www.worlded.org/) and [Kampuchean Action for Primary Education](http://www.kapekh.org) (KAPE), two partner NGOs dedicated to improving access to education, launched a program for 2,000 children in Kampong Cham to improve literacy rates through the use of new technology.
