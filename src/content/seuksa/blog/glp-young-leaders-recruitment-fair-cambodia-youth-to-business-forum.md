**Topic: Young Leaders Recruitment Fair &amp; Cambodia Youth to Business Forum
Date: Sunday, 1 July 2012 13:00 until 17:00** 
**Venue: Norton University New Campus (Accross Chroy Changva Bridge)**

## Programs:

Want to meet your future employers?
Want to express your thoughts about Education and Entrepreneurship in Cambodia?
Want to hear what do the representatives from the business world think?

By joining this YLRF &amp; Y2B 2012, you will an opportunity to:

- Meet the top companies in Cambodia and interact with their representatives to discover your opportunities to be employed
- Join a series of workshops delivered by experienced people from different backgrounds.
- Join the discussion and explore issue &amp; solutions on the topic of "The role of Education and development of Entrepreneurs in Cambodia" with representatives of youth and our panel from the business world.
- Create your network with the other 1000 talented youth in Cambodia

**Deadline: 25th June, 2012.**
