I will try to make it more better in the future but at least, it's a started one to play with.

### App Description

Seuksa.com is a educational content website in Cambodia. **Seuksa** is the word in Khmer which means "**study**" or "**learn**" in English.

Seuksa.com comes with following sub sites:

- Inspiration Quotes
- Q&amp;A site
- Stories in Khmer
- Our blog
- and others
