Soon in this week, on 22, 23 and 24 June 2012 at Koh Pich (Diamond Island), Phnom Penh ~ 8th Career forum is going to happen with a topic: "Green Economy" in its brochure.

According to Sabay News' report, there will be around 60 companies and NGOs waiting all candidates to apply for any job available for their organizations.

The event is FREE of charge for participants but companies with booth require to pay.

Career Forum is an annual event, created in May 2005 by CCFC. Every year in June, thousands of participants gather in front of professional stands and numerous public lectures. It is the result of a special join effort between private and public sectors. Indeed, Career Forum gathers companies, NGO, different kind of organizations and universities.
