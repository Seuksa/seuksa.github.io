[Guiness Word Record website](http://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/records-3000/longest-alphabet/) stated Khmer Alphabet, the longest one:

> The language with the most letters is Khmer (Cambodian), with 74 (including some without any current use).

According to the website [Everything2.com](http://everything2.com/index.pl?node=Khmer), the Guiness book recorded this record in 1995.

Khmer alphabet consists 33 consonants, 23 vowels and 12 independent vowels.
