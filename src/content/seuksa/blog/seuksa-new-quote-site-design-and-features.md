It's been sometime, we leave the improvement and content free without much input.

We take an opportunity of my free-time to complete a re-design of quote site (<http://quote.seuksa.com>) with few included features.

#### Better readable

White, Grey &amp; Green in few places

Spaces..

Seuksa focuses on content readers, we love and encourage people to read and share from opinion to information.
Even we are not a big organization but we keep our vision by doing steps by steps with our existing effort.

[![quote-new-home](/media/seuksa/blog/7-504/quote-new-home.png)](/media/seuksa/blog/7-504/quote-new-home.png)

We have new home page for quote site and when view the single quote, more spaces. We remove all sidebars that may disturb you from reading and reflection on the quote's content.

#### Quote by Author

We start putting each quote into correct author, of course, we need sometimes to move our old quote and we also try to respect our source of content to keep tracks of where we get the quote from. Some quote we receive in one language, we keep to have both: English + Khmer from our own translation and correcting by your comment if we missed some points.

[![quote-by-author](/media/seuksa/blog/7-504/quote-by-author.png)](/media/seuksa/blog/7-504/quote-by-author.png)

#### Better sharing

As you may notice, we enable "embed me" where you can embed our quote directly into your website beside we have QR code and other sharing to social media.

Your comment, reading, share and like is our encouragement to keep having better content for our society. We commit to bring inspiration content to keep your reading the best as ever.
